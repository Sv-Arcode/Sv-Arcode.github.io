Research & projects

MISID — Multimodal Hidden-Intent Recognition

Co-first author · NAIS Lab · Sep 2025–Present · Under review at AAAI 2027

A benchmark of 3,962 aligned audiovisual utterance segments from strategic-deception games. I co-designed the annotation scheme, independently designed and conducted the inter-annotator agreement assessment, and contributed to model evaluations and ablation studies.

I co-designed the FRACTAM reasoning framework and independently designed its modal-decoupling component, which generates separate audio and visual descriptions without speech transcripts to preserve potential cross-modal inconsistencies.

FRACTAM improved mean Hidden Intent Inference Score from 34.22 to 42.79 across ten LLMs (0–100 scale).

Visual Token Compression for Vision-Language Models

Final-year project · Supervisor: Prof. Fangxin Wang · Sep 2026–Present

I study client-side compression of visual information for bandwidth-limited transmission to server-side vision-language models. I adapted a question-independent, MetaCompress-based module for image question answering and compare learned compression with random and uniform token selection. I also evaluate visual-token selection for video question answering.

AffectiveArt — Artwork Emotion Understanding

Team lead · ACM Multimedia 2026 Grand Challenge, Track 2 · May–Jun 2026

I led a three-member team, designed the system architecture, and independently developed task-specific weighted voting for emotion, valence, and arousal classification. I also fine-tuned visual models to predict attribute salience for emotion-description generation.

Our system placed 5th among 14 teams, with hidden-test accuracies of 79.2% for emotion, 88.3% for valence, and 94.0% for arousal.

Interpretable Butterfly Classification

Course project lead · ECE4513 · Summer 2025 · Code ↗

Led a three-member team to develop a four-convolutional-layer CNN for ten visually similar species (83.23% test accuracy). Implemented Class Activation Mapping from scratch and used PCA to compare feature-map activation patterns.
