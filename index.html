About

I am a final-year B.Eng. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Computer Engineering stream) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen), and an undergraduate researcher in the Network and Artificial Intelligence System (NAIS) Lab, advised by Prof. Fangxin Wang.

My work centers on making large models understand people more faithfully: I study how multimodal large language models recognize hidden intent in long, strategic interactions, and how they interpret affect in fine-grained visual domains. I also care about whether model decisions can be explained and trusted.

Research & Projects

MISID: Hidden-Intent Recognition in Strategic Deception Games AAAI 2027 · Under Review

NAIS Lab, CUHK-Shenzhen · Co-first author · Sep 2025 – Present

Built the first multimodal, multi-turn benchmark for hidden-intent recognition in strategic deception games — 3,962 temporally aligned audiovisual utterance segments with a two-tier annotation scheme linking utterance-level affect to long-range factual evidence. Benchmarked state-of-the-art LLMs/MLLMs and co-designed FRACTAM, a "Decouple–Anchor–Reason" baseline with explicit cross-modal evidence chains.

AffectiveArt Challenge 2026 (ACM Multimedia Grand Challenge, Track 2) Rank 5 / 14

Team lead · May 2026 – Jun 2026

Led a 3-member team in multidimensional art-emotion understanding. Designed an end-to-end pipeline coupling FAB-G salience filtering over five formal art cues with a four-model fine-tuned ensemble (Gemma-4-12B, Qwen3.5-9B, SigLIP2-giant, DINOv3) and a bidirectional classification–description feedback loop, reaching 94.0% arousal accuracy and an overall score of 0.8236 on the official leaderboard.

Interpretable Butterfly Species Classification 83.23% accuracy

ECE4513 Image Processing and Computer Vision, CUHK-Shenzhen · Project lead · Summer 2025 · code

Trained a 4-layer CNN (32→256 filters) to classify 10 visually similar butterfly species, and implemented Class Activation Mapping from scratch alongside PCA feature-space visualization to trace each prediction to biologically meaningful wing regions. Authored an IEEE conference-format technical report on the interpretability framework.
